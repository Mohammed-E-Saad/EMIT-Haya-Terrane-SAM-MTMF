# EMIT-Haya-Terrane-SAM-MTMF
Manuscript: An EMIT-Based SAM–MTMF Workflow for Alteration-Related Exploration Targeting and Matched-Site Validation in the Haya Terrane, NE Sudan
Journal: Journal of African Earth Sciences
Manuscript ID: AES16883
Repository status: Under preparation. Data and code have not yet been released.

Study overview

This study evaluates NASA EMIT Level-2A imaging spectroscopy for alteration-related exploration targeting in the Haya Terrane, northeastern Sudan. Six scene-derived spectral families were identified, three of which were used for operational alteration-related targeting with Spectral Angle Mapper (SAM) and Mixture-Tuned Matched Filtering (MTMF). The outputs were assessed separately and as a candidate union (SAM OR MTMF) and strict detector-agreement subset (SAM AND MTMF).

Site-level evaluation compared 19 artisanal gold-mining sites with 19 geologically matched non-mining controls. These comparisons evaluate spatial association with known mining activity; they do not constitute field confirmation of alteration mineralogy or gold mineralization.

Planned repository content

The repository is being prepared to document the reproducible analytical workflow. Subject to checks for completeness, ownership and publication permissions, it may include:

Processing notes and classification thresholds.

Shareable scene-derived spectral endmember data.

Scripts for site-level statistics and sensitivity analyses.

Aggregated validation results and supplementary tables.

Documentation of data provenance and reproducibility limits.

No raw mining-site or control-site coordinates should be uploaded without explicit permission to publish them. Files that have not been checked or released are not represented here as available.
